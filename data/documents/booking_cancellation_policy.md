# Booking and Cancellation Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how Ashwin Logistics creates, modifies, reschedules, and cancels customer shipments. It applies to domestic and international bookings across road freight, air freight, sea freight, warehousing, and distribution services.

All booking references use the format BKG-XXXX. Shipment references use SHP-XXXX and customer references use CUS-XXXX.

For claims related to cargo damage, loss, shortage, or service failure, Ashwin Logistics internal policy requires that claims must normally be reported within 7 calendar days of delivery or discovery of the incident.

## Booking Creation

A booking is created when a customer requests a shipment move and the Ashwin Logistics team confirms service availability, route, cost, and pickup timing. A booking is not considered final until the required information is collected and the pickup date is accepted by both the customer and the service team.

### Required Booking Information

For each booking, Ashwin Logistics requires the following minimum information:

- customer ID, such as CUS-3001
- origin and destination addresses
- pickup date and preferred pickup window
- service type, such as road, air, or sea
- cargo description and quantity
- special handling requirements
- contact details for pickup and delivery
- documentation requirements for international shipments

If required information is missing, the booking may remain in BOOKED status but should not be considered operationally ready for pickup until the information is complete.

## Booking Modification and Rescheduling

Booking modifications are allowed when the change does not affect service readiness, compliance, or route allocation. Common examples include adjusting the pickup time, updating the delivery address, or changing the cargo description before the vehicle is dispatched.

A pickup reschedule is handled as a booking modification. Ashwin Logistics may honor a reschedule if the revised time is still operationally feasible and the service lane remains available. If multiple attempts are needed, the case may be escalated to Operations for route or capacity review.

## Cancellation Policy

Ashwin Logistics applies a time-based cancellation policy based on the planned pickup time. The cancellation rules are applied to bookings before pickup. Once a shipment has been picked up, it cannot be treated as a normal cancellation and must follow the return or exception process.

### Cancellation Decision Table

| Time relative to scheduled pickup | Cancellation result | Fee | Approval requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| More than 4 hours before scheduled pickup | Cancellation allowed | No cancellation fee | None |
| Between 2 and 4 hours before scheduled pickup | Cancellation allowed | Standard cancellation fee applies | Normal operations review |
| Less than 2 hours before scheduled pickup | Cancellation requires Operations approval | Standard cancellation fee may apply if approved | Operations approval required |
| After pickup | Shipment cannot be treated as a normal cancellation | Not applicable | Return/exception process |

### Cancellation Rules by Time Window

- More than 4 hours before scheduled pickup: cancellations are allowed with no cancellation fee.
- Between 2 and 4 hours before scheduled pickup: cancellations are allowed and the standard cancellation fee applies.
- Less than 2 hours before scheduled pickup: cancellations require Operations approval. Support cannot independently guarantee cancellation.
- After pickup: the booking cannot be resolved as an ordinary cancellation. The shipment must be handled through the return or exception workflow.

### Standard Cancellation Fee

The standard cancellation fee is a fixed charge based on service type, lane, and operational cost. It is not a penalty for customer inconvenience; it reflects the cost already assigned for pickup planning, resource allocation, and dispatch readiness. The exact amount is set in the applicable service rate sheet and must be referenced by the operations or billing team, not by customer support representatives.

## Approval Requirements

A cancellation submitted less than 2 hours before scheduled pickup must be reviewed by Operations before the cancellation is confirmed. The outcome depends on whether the pickup team has already been assigned, whether the vehicle is en route, or whether the shipment has already been physically accepted.

Support responsibilities include:

- confirming the original pickup time and booking status
- collecting the customer request and reason for cancellation
- checking whether the request falls within the cancellation windows
- escalating to Operations when the request is within the less-than-2-hours window or when pickup has already occurred

Operations responsibilities include:

- approving or denying short-notice cancellations
- confirming whether the shipment has already been picked up
- deciding whether the shipment should move to EXCEPTION, RETURNED, or remain in BOOKED/PICKUP_SCHEDULED state
- confirming billing implications if a standard cancellation fee applies

## Exceptions and Edge Cases

Ashwin Logistics may apply a different treatment in the following cases:

- a vehicle has already been dispatched and cannot be redirected without cost
- a shipment contains temperature-sensitive, hazardous, or high-risk materials that require special handling
- a customer requests cancellation after the booked pickup time but before physical collection has occurred
- a shipment is in a customs or compliance hold and cannot be legally moved or cancelled without additional documentation

In these situations, the case should be reviewed by Operations and may require manager approval or a documented exception note.

## Support Guidance for Customer Questions

Customer support staff must follow this policy exactly and must not promise a cancellation outcome without checking the booking status and time window. For example:

- A customer calls 6 hours before pickup asking to cancel: support may confirm that the cancellation is allowed and no fee applies.
- A customer calls 3 hours before pickup asking to cancel: support may confirm the cancellation is allowed, but the standard cancellation fee applies.
- A customer calls 45 minutes before pickup asking to cancel: support must escalate to Operations because approval is required.
- A customer calls after pickup: support must explain that the shipment is no longer treated as a normal cancellation and must follow the return or exception process.

## Examples

### Example 1: Same-day cancellation with no fee

A booking for BKG-2010 is scheduled for 10:00 AM. The customer requests cancellation at 5:30 AM on the same day. The request is more than 4 hours before pickup, so the cancellation is allowed with no cancellation fee.

### Example 2: Short-notice cancellation with fee

A booking for BKG-2014 is scheduled for 6:00 PM. The customer requests cancellation at 3:00 PM. The request is between 2 and 4 hours before pickup, so the cancellation is allowed and the standard cancellation fee applies.

### Example 3: Less than 2 hours before pickup

A booking for BKG-2022 is scheduled for 8:00 PM. The customer requests cancellation at 7:30 PM. The request is less than 2 hours before pickup, so cancellation requires Operations approval. Support cannot independently promise the cancellation.

### Example 4: After pickup

A shipment with SHP-1028 was picked up at 8:15 AM. The customer requests cancellation at 9:00 AM. This request is not handled as a normal booking cancellation. The shipment must move through the return or exception workflow.
